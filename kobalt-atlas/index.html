Dejan de verse.Two siblings, aged seven and nine, lose their parents in an accident. No relatives can care for both. Social services assess foster homes and assign each child to the first suitable one. They end up eighty kilometres apart. They stop seeing each other.

El sistema hizo su trabajo: cada niño está en un hogar seguro, supervisado, con cuidadores evaluados. No hubo negligencia ni violación de procedimiento. Ese sentimiento no es sentimentalismo. Es un diagnóstico preciso: quien escucha evalúa con una función que incluye el vínculo entre los hermanos; el sistema, no. Lo que faltaba era la inclusión de ese vínculo en lo que el sistema buscaba.That feeling is not sentimentality. It is a precise diagnosis: the listener evaluates with a function that includes the siblings' bond; the system does not. And this is the decisive part: the system knew they were siblings. It had their data, their ages, their history. The information was there. What was missing was the inclusion of that bond in what the system sought.

Comunicar no es enviar un paquete que llega intacto si el canal funciona. Since those frames almost never coincide, perfect agreement is improbable by structure, not by accident.

La fórmula convierte la comprensión en un problema de optimización: entre todo lo que puede concebir, el receptor elige la lectura que mejor sirve a su finalidad, dada la señal. That is why confirmation bias is no mysterious error: it is optimizing coherence instead of fidelity. And the same advice, given right after an argument, reads as an attack: the state of the relationship moves g, K and τ at once.

Lo inconcebible no es lo improbable.The inconceivable is not the improbable.

La escenaThe scene

Una persona con un prejuicio arraigado puede formular la frase «quizá me equivoco sobre ellos». La concibe. Pero no puede sostenerla: examinarla le cuesta demasiado. Averroes, en cambio, ni siquiera podía formular «teatro». Son dos bloqueos distintos, y ninguno se resuelve con más datos.A person with a deep-rooted prejudice can utter the sentence “maybe I am wrong about them”. They can conceive it. But they cannot hold it: examining it costs too much. Averroes, by contrast, could not even formulate “theatre”. These are two different blockages, and neither is solved with more data.

La ideaThe idea

En un marco bayesiano toda hipótesis, por improbable que sea, se puede evaluar. Aquí no: hay hipótesis que nunca entran en la evaluación porque el marco no las genera. Lo improbable pide evidencia. Lo inconcebible pide construir coordenadas nuevas. Lo intolerable pide ampliar, poco a poco, la sorpresa que se puede soportar.In a Bayesian frame every hypothesis, however improbable, can be evaluated. Not here: some hypotheses never enter the evaluation because the frame does not generate them. The improbable calls for evidence. The inconceivable calls for building new coordinates. The intolerable calls for widening, step by step, the surprise one can bear.

De ahí una definición precisa de claridad: un mensaje es claro para alguien si reduce el espacio de lecturas plausibles compatibles con la señal. La claridad es relativa a quien recibe, tiene costo y puede destruirse al simplificar: si se quitan las restricciones que cerraban el espacio, quedan más lecturas posibles, no menos. Transferir marco es dar las coordenadas para que el otro genere el concepto por sí mismo.Hence a precise definition of clarity: a message is clear for someone if it shrinks the space of plausible readings compatible with the signal. Clarity is relative to the receiver, has a cost and can be destroyed by simplifying: remove the constraints that closed the space and more readings remain possible, not fewer. Transferring a frame means giving the coordinates so the other can generate the concept themselves.

Y una estrategia contra la rigidez: explorar antes de juzgar. Generar entre tres y cinco alternativas genuinas antes de evaluar rompe el cierre prematuro; cada alternativa es una pequeña excursión fuera de la zona cómoda, y repetirlas la ensancha.And a strategy against rigidity: explore before judging. Generating three to five genuine alternatives before evaluating breaks premature closure; each alternative is a small excursion outside the comfort zone, and repeating them widens it.

It is multidimensional, and each dimension has its own geometry: it starts from deprivation, rises toward flourishing and can lose value through saturation. The candidate function V(D,C) = −C·D ln D —the individual term of Shannon entropy— gathers those properties: infinite sensitivity at deprivation, concavity, a zero that does not destroy the other dimensions, and an interior maximum.

Dos consecuencias tienen fuerza propia. Con recursos limitados y contextos iguales, el óptimo reparte por igual: la equidad emerge de la concavidad, no se impone como axioma. Y si el máximo está en D* = 1/e, disponer de recursos no obliga a consumirlos: el excedente queda libre para otras necesidades. Los umbrales —una sigmoide sobre cada dimensión— formalizan lo que llamamos derechos: niveles bajo los cuales una entidad colapsa de forma casi irreversible.Two consequences carry weight of their own. With limited resources and equal contexts, the optimum spreads equally: equity emerges from concavity, it is not imposed as an axiom. And if the maximum lies at D* = 1/e, having resources does not oblige us to use them up: the surplus stays free for other needs. Thresholds —a sigmoid on each dimension— formalize what we call rights: levels below which an entity collapses almost irreversibly.

No es una ecuación universal de la vida. It is a candidate with verifiable properties, which requires rebuilding with each entity which dimensions matter and how they are lived.
